Using your travel library

Open the full user guide →

Trip links automatically choose the PC or smartphone presentation. Use “View” to switch the overview and plan links between Auto, PC and Smartphone. On a phone, explore Map and Guide. Search and the expandable Trip plan sit above the map; tap a location for its preview, then choose Open guide for details.

Open a trip for its recommended itinerary, map, stays and activities. Inside each stop, use Activities, Stay options, Local map, Conditions and Sources to find the practical detail.

Recommendations and approximate travel times are planning advice. Reviews describe the named attraction or property; prices and schedules remain subject to their recorded checks. A presentation update does not refresh the research.

Saved map backgrounds, supplied photographs and descriptions work offline. Live weather, new map detail and external booking pages need internet. Use the dated source records inside each plan when checking what is current.

Browser updates normally keep access. Persistence can reduce automatic eviction, but cannot survive deliberate deletion of all site data.
